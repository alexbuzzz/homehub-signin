Home Hub — privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Home Hub is a private family planner: a tablet on our kitchen wall and our family's phones. It is not offered to anyone outside our family.

What it uses from Google

  • The list of your Google calendars, so you can choose which ones to show on the wall.
  • The events in the calendars you choose: their title, description, place, time and repeat — to show them on the wall, and to add, change or delete events you make or change there.

Home Hub asks only for these two permissions (calendar.events and calendar.calendarlist.readonly).

Where it is kept

Your sign-in token stays only on the family's wall tablet. Events are shown on the family's own devices through our home server. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No analytics.

The sign-in page

This website only passes you on to Google's own sign-in and then shows a one-time code as a QR for the tablet's camera. It keeps nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Removing access

Disconnect your calendar on the tablet (Settings → Family → your name → Google Calendar → Disconnect), or remove Home Hub at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either stops all access at once.
